# Mr. Couch: spoken demo script (about 2.5 minutes)

You talk to him by voice and narrate over it. Two kinds of lines below:

- **🎙️ TO CAMERA**: your commentary, in your normal voice.
- **🛋️ TO COUCH**: hold **Ctrl+Alt+Space**, say the line, let go.

Rules that keep it smooth:

- Don't start with "Hey Mr. Couch". Just say the command. The hotkey already means you're talking to him.
- Wait until he finishes talking before you narrate. Talking over him looks messy in the edit.
- Lines marked ⏳ go to the AI and take about 3 to 6 seconds. Fill that gap with commentary, or cut it in the edit.
- His replies change a little every time. Don't wait for the exact words written here.

---

## Before you hit record

- `npm start`, then wait 2 minutes for the file scan.
- Tray: **Sim home OFF** (scene 7 needs "nothing connected"). **Speak file names** ON. **Voice** ON.
- Play anything on YouTube Music once in your browser, then pause it. This lets it autoplay later.
- Put your `DLCA assignment` PDF in Documents, Downloads or Desktop.
- **Off camera, set up his memory:** say "ask me my preferences again" and answer the five questions. Then say "remember my favourite movie is Interstellar".
- Do one dry run of scene 4 off camera so Windows doesn't show a security dialog on the PDF.
- Hide desktop icons, close other windows, Windows Do not disturb on, laptop brightness around 70 percent.
- Record the laptop screen (not an external monitor) with Win+Alt+R or OBS. Mic on.

## 1. Intro (0:00 to 0:15)

**On screen:** clean desktop. He's asleep on his couch in the corner.

🎙️ **TO CAMERA:**
> Hi, I'm Vin. This little guy in the corner is Mr. Couch. He lives on my desktop, he's lazy, and he does my errands. On a phone you just say "hey Siri, do this". On a laptop you're still clicking through folders and tabs. So I built him.

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "Hello."

**He says something like:** "Hey. Good evening, I guess."

## 2. He's got a mood (0:15 to 0:28)

🎙️ **TO CAMERA:**
> He's not a search bar. He has a personality. Watch.

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "Hey, can you do something for me?"

**He says something like:** "Alright. But I'm tired. What is it?"

🎙️ **TO CAMERA:**
> He complains every time. Then he does it anyway.

## 3. YouTube, volume, pause (0:28 to 0:48)

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "Play lo-fi beats on YouTube." ⏳

**On screen:** his own little player pops up and starts the video.

🎙️ **TO CAMERA:**
> No browser, no searching, no clicking the first result. He finds it and plays it.

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "Volume 30." Then: "Pause."

🎙️ **TO CAMERA:**
> And I can control it without touching anything.

## 4. Opening a file (0:48 to 1:02)

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "Open my DLCA assignment in my browser." ⏳

**On screen:** the PDF opens.

🎙️ **TO CAMERA:**
> I didn't tell him which folder or the full file name. He knows what's on my laptop and just opens it. Everything is indexed on my machine. Nothing gets uploaded.

## 5. YouTube Music (1:02 to 1:15)

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "Play some Tame Impala on YouTube Music." ⏳

**On screen:** YouTube Music opens in the browser and plays. The popup shows what he found.

🎙️ **TO CAMERA:**
> Songs go to YouTube Music. Videos go to his own player. He picks the right one by himself.

## 6. Reading my screen (1:15 to 1:32)

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "What's on my screen?" ⏳

**On screen:** he says the short version, and the popup opens with the full answer.

🎙️ **TO CAMERA:**
> He only looks at my screen when I ask him to. One screenshot, it never gets saved, and he can't click anything based on what he sees. He just tells me.

## 7. Being honest: lights, dimming, brightness (1:32 to 1:55)

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "Turn off the LED."

**He says, instantly:** "No smart devices connected right now. I can dim your screen instead. Say "dim the screen"."

🎙️ **TO CAMERA:**
> I don't have smart lights here, and he doesn't pretend I do. He tells me straight away and offers something he *can* do.

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "Dim the screen."

**On screen:** the screen goes darker.

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "Brighten the screen." Then: "Reduce the brightness of my screen."

**On screen:** the actual laptop brightness drops.

🎙️ **TO CAMERA:**
> That one's my real screen brightness, not a filter. And if I plug in Philips Hue, Shelly or Home Assistant, the same sentence turns off real lights.

## 8. Notes and reminders (1:55 to 2:10)

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "Take a note: buy chips and juices."

**On screen:** Notepad opens with the note.

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "Remind me in 10 minutes to submit the project."

🎙️ **TO CAMERA:**
> Small stuff. But that's what I actually use every day. And these are instant, because there's no AI involved for simple commands.

## 9. Goodnight (2:10 to 2:20)

🛋️ **TO COUCH:** "Close yourself."

**He says** a goodbye line and the app closes. (It also puts your screen brightness overlay back to normal.)

## 10. Outro (2:20 to 2:30)

🎙️ **TO CAMERA:**
> That's Mr. Couch. He runs on Gemma, so he can work fully offline on your own laptop. He's lazy, a little rude, and he gets things done. Thanks for watching.

**End card (3 seconds):**
> **Mr. Couch.** Gemma · Mastra · ElevenLabs voice. Runs locally.

## Editing (10 minutes)

1. Cut the AI waiting gaps down to about half a second.
2. Add each spoken command as a caption at the top of the screen, so people watching muted can follow.
3. Keep the Tame Impala and lo-fi clips short (a few seconds each) so the video doesn't get muted for copyright. Use free stock music under the intro and outro.
4. Export 1080p.

## If something goes wrong

- He mishears you: speak a bit slower and redo that line. Don't fight it on camera.
- "Brain is busy" or a long wait: wait 10 seconds and redo the scene.
- File not found: the scan wasn't finished. Wait a minute and redo.
- "This monitor won't let me": you're on an external monitor. Record on the laptop screen.
- Two songs playing at once: say "pause" (stops his player) or pause the browser tab yourself.
