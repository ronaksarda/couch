Couch

A lazy guy on a driving couch who does your computer chores. He complains. He does them anyway.

Download for Windows Windows 10 and 11. Free.

This is a toy room. The real one lives in the corner of your screen and drives to your actual files, videos and smart lights.

His job description

  • Finds files by what's inside them. "The lease from last month" works, even if it's named scan_0042.pdf. "Two leases. Pick one."
  • Puts on something you like. Asks what you watch once, then picks from it. Thumbnails pop up, you click one. "Some options. Choose. I will wait."
  • Looks things up on a site you name. "Pricing page on stripe.com" gets you the actual page, checked. "Hold on, lemme look."
  • Runs the lights, AC and plugs. Hue, Home Assistant, Shelly, Tasmota. No smart home? There's a pretend one to boss around. "Pressing the button. Very hard."
  • Reads your screen, only when you ask. "What's this error?" and he takes one look. Nothing is kept. "Mm. Working on it. Allegedly."
  • Timers, reminders, notes, dimming the screen, opening apps. "Done. You owe me a snack."

Not in his job description

  • Deleting your files
  • Paying for anything
  • Typing passwords
  • Sending messages as you
  • Running commands
  • Changing system settings

Anything that changes stuff, like moving a file, downloading, or unlocking a door, waits for you to tap Yes. Saying "yes" out loud doesn't count. He checked.

Manners

He does your chores, so be decent. Swear at the printer all you like. Insult him once and he grumbles and still does it. Twice in half an hour and he goes on strike until you say sorry. Say thanks and he notices.

Getting him home

  1. Run the installer. Windows may say "unknown publisher" because the app is new: click More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Setup opens. Paste a free Gemini key from Google AI Studio. That's his brain, and it's fast.
  3. Voice (ElevenLabs), web search (SerpApi) and page reading (Browserbase) are optional. Skip any; the rest still works.
  4. Tick the folders he may look in, answer five quick questions or skip them.
  5. Click him, or press Ctrl Alt Space, and ask.

He idles at a few frames a second and indexes files slowly in the background, so an older laptop stays usable.

Where your stuff goes

Your keys stay in %APPDATA%\couch\.env on your PC. File names and contents go to the brain only if you tick that in setup. Voice goes to ElevenLabs only while the mic button is on. Search words go to SerpApi, page addresses to Browserbase. Your taste profile stays on your PC unless you add your own MongoDB. The tray has "Forget me", which wipes it.
